## Questions, checks, and results

### Selection and evidence

**Question:** Why did you select Chipotle when fiscal-year 2025 comparable restaurant sales declined 1.7% and transactions declined 2.9%? Can you show me the 10-K evidence?

**Check performed:** Kara and I opened Chipotle's 10-K and reviewed the MD&A disclosure. The filing supported the reported 1.7% comparable-sales decline, 2.9% transaction decline, and 334 restaurant openings.

**Result:** The evidence supported Kara's claim that Chipotle remains a familiar, researchable company with accessible SEC filings and comparable companies, while also showing that recent same-store demand weakened.

### Model and valuation

**Question:** Your DCF gives $21.69 per share and your pro-forma gives $19.65 per share. Why are the values different?

**Check performed:** We traced the assumptions and valuation conventions used by each model.

**Result:** The values use different cash-flow and discounting approaches: the DCF uses FCFF and WACC, while the pro-forma uses FCFE and cost of equity. Identical values are not expected when the methods, cash-flow definitions, and discount rates differ.

### Sensitivity and interpretation

**Question:** You say revenue growth is your most important driver. Does that conclusion depend on the range you chose?

**Check performed:** Kara showed that revenue growth and gross margin were each tested using the same plus-or-minus one-percentage-point change.

**Result:** Testing equal percentage-point changes makes the comparison more consistent. The driver ranking still applies only to those tested ranges; the sensitivity table does not assign probabilities to the outcomes.
