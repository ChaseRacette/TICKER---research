# Lab 09 Prework — Three-Statement Pro-Forma

## My explanation before building

For the ABG training case, the three judgments that carry the valuation are:

1. **Revenue growth** — especially unit sales and same-store sales, because revenue is the starting point for the income statement and determines the scale of inventory, receivables, and operating expenses.
2. **Operating profitability** — gross margin and selling, general, and administrative expense as a share of revenue, because small changes in dealer margins can produce large changes in operating income and cash flow.
3. **Capital intensity and working capital** — inventory, capital expenditures, and the financing required to support them, because growth consumes cash before it becomes value to shareholders.

Cash is the last line the model computes because it is an outcome, not a plug. The income statement produces net income; the balance-sheet changes and noncash expenses turn that into cash flow; financing and investing flows then determine ending cash. Computing cash last lets the balance sheet test the model. If assets do not equal liabilities plus equity, the model contains a formula or linkage bug—not a valid forecast.

It is not dealer inventory financing, but it is the line most likely to change the relationship between reported operating income, cash flow, and future margins.
