## MD&A: operating performance and business drivers

Microsoft Cloud revenue increased 27% to $214.4 billion in fiscal 2026. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 34.

Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 34.

Intelligent Cloud revenue increased $31.5 billion, or 30%, and Azure and other cloud services revenue increased 41%. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 38.

Microsoft Cloud gross-margin percentage decreased to 66%; the company attributed the decline to continued AI-infrastructure investment, growing AI-product usage, and a sales-mix shift toward Azure, partly offset by efficiency gains. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 37.

Intelligent Cloud cost of revenue increased 44%, which the company attributed to AI-infrastructure investment supporting customer demand; Intelligent Cloud operating income increased 28%. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 38.

Productivity and Business Processes revenue increased 16% and operating income increased 20%; Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue grew 17%. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 38.

More Personal Computing revenue decreased 1%, including a 5% decrease in XBOX content and services revenue. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 39.

Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments totaled $76.8 billion at June 30, 2026, compared with $94.6 billion at June 30, 2025. Microsoft stated that it expects continued investment in datacenters, cloud offerings, and AI training and infrastructure. **Source:** Microsoft Corporation, Form 10-K, Item 7 - MD&A, p. 42.
