# Cathie Wood Panel Skill

Use the shared `panel_skill.py` rows. Cathie Wood's approach emphasizes disruptive innovation, adoption, and long-duration growth. The 10-K-only panel can test revenue growth from the first displayed year to the last and positive operating cash flow in every displayed year. It cannot establish a disruptive-innovation thesis or adoption evidence from SEC company facts alone; that check must stay `not known`. Do not estimate or fill this gap. Write only from printed values.
