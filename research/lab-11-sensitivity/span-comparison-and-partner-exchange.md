## Causal explanation using the actual results

**Revenue-growth link:** changing the five annual revenue-growth inputs changes revenue, then gross profit and operating profit, then net income and FCFE after the model recalculates working capital and capex. Across the stated lower-to-higher revenue path, FY2031E operating profit ranges from $243,208.6m to $291,224.2m and FCFE from $158,744.0m to $175,575.4m.

**AI/cloud capex link:** changing capex as a percent of revenue changes capital spending, PP&E, depreciation, cash, and FCFE. In this simplified model, it does not change FY2031E operating profit because capex is treated as a cash investment rather than an additional operating-income expense; across the stated lower-to-higher capex path, FCFE ranges from $186,247.0m to $147,721.4m and value per share from $270.90 to $206.53.
