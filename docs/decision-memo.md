# Decision Memo — Racette Investments Portfolio Pilot

**Decision:** **PILOT** the Racette Investments portfolio process for a 15-year, medium-high-risk investor.

## Mandate

The process seeks CPI + 5% annualized, modeled as a 7.5% nominal planning objective at 2.5% CPI. It maximizes historical expected return subject to a 15% estimated-volatility ceiling, a 90% five-business-day liquidity floor, at least 5% in Treasury reserve, and no more than 15% combined alternatives. Private credit is capped at 10% and is treated as less liquid.

## Evidence from the dated output

The 2026-11-17 live Yahoo Finance run used 60 completed monthly returns from October 2021 through October 2026. The equal-weight baseline failed the mandate and produced a 5.99% historical expected return with 9.37% estimated volatility. The 70/20/10 benchmark passed at 7.80% and 11.97%.

The constrained Racette recommendation passed at a 9.25% historical expected return and 13.18% estimated volatility: 5% Treasury reserve, 10% investment-grade bonds, 80% global equity index, 0% private-credit proxy, and 5% listed infrastructure. Full dated output appears in [decision-output.md](decision-output.md).

## Recommendation and monitoring

Pilot the process rather than immediately adopt it for real allocation. During the pilot, review the ACWI ten-month trend monitor, liquidity classification, data availability, sleeve drift, and the changed-input stress result. Rebalance only in an annual review after a sleeve drifts more than five percentage points from its target.

The pilot implementation range is 75–80% global equity, 5–10% Treasury reserve, 10–15% investment-grade bonds, 0–5% listed infrastructure, and 0–5% private-credit proxy, with total alternatives limited to 10% during the pilot. The current target is 80% global equity, 5% Treasury reserve, 10% investment-grade bonds, 5% listed infrastructure, and 0% private-credit proxy. Any move into private credit requires separate liquidity and redemption-term review because BIZD is only a public proxy.

Pause new allocations and review the process if no feasible portfolio can meet the CPI + 5% objective while passing every guardrail. A 25% drawdown also triggers committee review of liquidity, assumptions, and mandate compliance; it does not automatically cause a sale.
